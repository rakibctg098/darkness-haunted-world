ABOUT

DARKNESS: THE HAUNTED WORLD is an atmospheric 3D first-person horror survival experience. You awaken inside the locked corridors of St. Jude Psychiatric Hospital. Evade The Surgeon, locate the Generator Room Key, restore emergency electrical power, and escape through the security blast gate before your battery dies.

Controls: Left screen touch moves your character with the virtual joystick. Right screen drag rotates your camera look. Use the action buttons to toggle your flashlight, sprint, crouch, and interact.
